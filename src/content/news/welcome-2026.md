Registration for the **2026 Timpanogos Invitational** is officially open. The meet returns to Lakeside Park in Vineyard, Utah on Friday, September 4, 2026, with two sessions (AM and PM) capped at 45 teams each.

To reserve your team's spot, sign up one runner through RunnerCard — links are on the [Register section](#register) of this page. Registration closes Tuesday, September 1 at 10:00 PM.

Questions? Email Jody Benson at [jodylbenson@alpinedistrict.org](mailto:jodylbenson@alpinedistrict.org).

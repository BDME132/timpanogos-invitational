The Course

The Timpanogos Invitational course is a true racer's dream — 3.1 miles of speed set beneath the stunning backdrop of Mt. Timpanogos. The course is 99% grass with minimal concrete, flat and fast, purpose-built for personal bests and breakthrough performances.

Notable Alumni

Champions Who Raced Here

Course Record · 15:02
Jackson Spencer
Herriman High School · 2025
Gatorade National Boys Cross Country Player of the Year (2025–26) and Nike Cross Nationals champion. Spencer swept both premier national XC titles, went undefeated all season, and set the all-time course record at Woodward Park (14:16). He holds the Timpanogos Invitational boys course record (15:02) and is committed to BYU.
Course Record · 17:38
Jane Hedengren
Timpview High School · 2024 & 2025
Gatorade National Girls Cross Country Player of the Year (2024–25), Nike Cross Nationals champion, and three-time Utah 5A state champion. Hedengren set U.S. high school records in the 5,000m (15:13) and the mile (4:26). She is the two-time individual champion at the Timpanogos Invitational and holds the girls course record (17:38). Now competing at BYU.
Daniel Simmons
American Fork High School · 2023
Two-time Gatorade National Boys Cross Country Player of the Year (2022–23 and 2023–24) — only the third athlete ever to win back-to-back. Simmons set the U.S. high school indoor 5,000m record (13:38) and the outdoor 5,000m national record (13:25). He was a two-time Utah 6A state champion and two-time NXR Southwest champion, and signed with BYU.
Rory Linkletter
Herriman High School · 2013
2013 Timpanogos Invitational individual champion. Linkletter became one of Canada's greatest distance runners — a 2024 Paris Olympian, two-time World Championships marathoner, and Canadian half marathon record holder (59:49). A six-time All-American at BYU, he owns the second-fastest marathon time in Canadian history (2:06:49).
Raygan Peterson
Timpanogos High School · 2022
2023 national champion in the girls 2,000m steeplechase at Nike Outdoor Nationals, run at Hayward Field in Eugene. Peterson was also an All-American in 2022, a cross country state champion (2021), and a multiple-time 4x400 and 4x800 state champion. She now runs cross country and track at BYU.
Tayson Echohawk
Orem High School · 2022
2022 Timpanogos Invitational individual champion and Utah 5A state champion. Echohawk ran the fastest 5,000m by a Utah high school runner at the time (14:24), was a two-time NXR Southwest runner-up, and earned All-American honors at RunningLane Nationals. He went on to run cross country at the University of Oregon.

About the Meet

The Timpanogos Invitational has been one of Utah's premier high school cross country invitationals since its founding in 2010. Held each fall at Lakeside Park in Vineyard, Utah — with the iconic peak of Mt. Timpanogos rising in the distance — the meet has grown into a destination event drawing 80+ teams and some of the nation's finest distance talent.

The course is legendary among Utah runners: 3.1 miles of fast, flat, 99% grass running purpose-built for personal bests. Year after year, athletes arrive chasing breakthrough performances — and many find them here.

The meet's alumni roll reads like a who's-who of elite American distance running. National Champion Jackson Spencer set the course record here. National Record Holder Jane Hedengren of Timpview has dominated the girls race. Steeple national champion Ragan Peterson cut his teeth on this course. The Timpanogos Invitational is where great teams race, champions compete, and memories are made.

Organized by Timpanogos High School and directed by Jody Benson, the meet prides itself on a well-run, athlete-first experience. In 2026, two sessions — AM and PM — will allow even more teams to compete while maintaining the high standard the invitational is known for.
